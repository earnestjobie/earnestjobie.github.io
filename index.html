Profile

// background & scope

Hi, I'm Jobie. I like engineering that has to actually work — not just look right on a drawing.

I'm a mechanical engineer based in Wiesbaden, Germany, currently a Research Assistant at Fraunhofer IWM, where I run structured materials and process characterisation experiments across six concurrent research projects — combining ANSYS and Abaqus FEA with real experimental validation.

My background spans three connected domains: precision mechanical design and manufacturing (CAD through CNC), semiconductor cleanroom fabrication (photolithography, etching, ion beam sputtering), and soft robotics / materials-driven system design (silicone moulding, electromechanical integration).

Before Fraunhofer, I led a soft actuator project at the livMatS Cluster of Excellence through three design generations — building every prototype myself and presenting the work as first-author research. At Neptune Lab, I fabricated micro-heater devices on silicon and glass wafers through a full semiconductor process flow.

My M.Tech thesis combined a cluster-based algorithm with machine learning to design dynamic vibration absorbers — early, genuine depth in data-driven modelling of physical systems.

Precision Bracket: CAD-to-Manufacture Validation

Fraunhofer IWM · 2025

Designed a structural bracket with integrated gusset in Onshape, then validated it through Abaqus structural and thermal FEA before manufacture — confirming deformation in the nanometre range and safe stress margins. The component was subsequently taken through CNC machining, completing a full simulation-to-physical-validation cycle.

Precision MEMS & Microfluidic Fabrication

Neptune Lab, University of Freiburg · 2023–2024

Fabricated micro-heater devices and MEMS actuators on silicon and glass wafers through a full semiconductor cleanroom process flow — photolithography, wet/dry etching (including reactive ion etching), ion beam sputtering, and thin film deposition.
